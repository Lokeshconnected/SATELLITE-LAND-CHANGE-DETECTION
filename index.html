Methodology

Understand how a result is made.

TerraScope keeps its process inspectable, from the satellite scene selected to the visual signal shown on the map.

01 / SOURCE

Sentinel‑2 imagery

For each year, the service selects a low-cloud Sentinel‑2 surface reflectance scene and creates a consistent RGB thumbnail for the selected area.

02 / MODEL

U-Net with visual cues

The trained U-Net estimates pixel-level change. RGB and texture differences provide additional evidence for the final screening map.

03 / REVIEW

Evidence before conclusions

Review the source images, acquisition metadata, map layer, region summaries, and exports together. The detector does not establish what caused a change.

Frequently asked questions

Designed for careful exploration.

A few practical details about coverage, results, and what you need to run an analysis.

What areas can I analyse?

Any location with available Sentinel‑2 imagery. Coverage, cloud conditions, and scene quality vary by place and date.

Why are results called “possible change”?

Image differences are a screening cue. They can be influenced by seasons, lighting, clouds, haze, and changes in acquisition conditions.

What is needed to run the live analysis?

The server needs a Google Earth Engine account and project configured for the Earth Engine API. See the README for local and Google Cloud setup.

Can I use this as an official land record?

No. TerraScope is a research and exploration tool. Its visual outputs are not legal evidence, a survey, or an authoritative land-cover product.
